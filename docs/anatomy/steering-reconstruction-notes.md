# Steering reconstruction — 14 October 2026

## Evidence and scope

[Mercedes' W17 specification](https://www.mercedesamgf1.com/f1-w17-2026-technical-specifications) confirms power-assisted rack-and-pinion steering and a carbon steering wheel. It does not disclose the rack casing, ratio, column joints, hardpoints or hydraulic routing.

[Moog's F1 PAS case study](https://www.moog.co.uk/markets/industrial/case-studies/PAS-Valves-For_F1.html) describes a miniature linear hydromechanical metering valve integrated with the rack. It supports a compact mechanical valve rather than an electric steering motor. Its historical vehicle-weight example is not a W17 specification. The model does not claim that its valve reproduces Moog's proprietary internal design or current Mercedes packaging.

[Woodward's technical information, manufacturer document hosted by its distributor](https://images.carid.com/woodward/items/pdf/technical-information.pdf), particularly the rack/cylinder parts lists and mechanical installation section, supplies generic references for shaft guides, retained bearings, piston/rod seals, bellows and mount clamps. These are construction references, not evidence of a Mercedes supplier or dimensions.

## Implemented construction

- Two straight shafts join the wheel boss to an aligned pinion through a retained cross-type universal joint. The previous curved tube has been removed. Upper and lower bearing supports meet the structural keel and forward survival cell.
- The previous checked tie-rod centres remain at X 1.48, Y 0.28, Z ±0.25 m. A toothed rack, pinion, guides, closed casing, piston, rod glands and seals fill the mechanism between them. Pleated bellows, clamps and locknuts cover the rack ends.
- A small valve block includes a sleeve, spool, metering lands and mechanical feedback linkage. Two hollow local lines terminate in drilled valve and cylinder fittings. Long pressure and return branches now connect the rear hydraulic manifold to the rack valve. Their reconstructed routes pass below the exhaust and around the cooling pipes, with seven structural carriers per line, retained unions, crimp sleeves and local passages in the steering bay. Accurate torque-sensing and internal valve galleries remain unfinished.
- The rack passes through separate side openings in a 2 mm reconstructed carbon laminate. The column has angled passages. Bore cleanup removes collapsed duplicate slivers while preserving necessary narrow faces; the existing closed-wall test remains enforced.
- The concealed underside and rear cap of the removable nose fairing no longer intersect the mechanism. Its exterior contour and the front crash-nose underside remain. The width regression now measures actual triangle cross-sections rather than assuming one fixed vertex ordering.
- The steering wheel and its controls belong to the steering assembly. The detail view uses the same geometry as the installed model, with a removable-housing control and a view facing the wheel display.

All dimensions, internal packaging and the indicated mechanical valve linkage are illustrative reconstruction. Steering motion, tyre articulation, valve metering, real assistance forces, impact behaviour and manufacturing tolerances are not simulated. This is not certified factory 1:1 CAD.

## Validation

The focused steering tests cover straight/coaxial shafts, connection to the existing wheel boss, bearing and rack supports, unchanged tie-rod hardpoints, full-radius clearance from surrounding meshes, terminated and smoothly curved local fluid pipes, actual fitting bores and shared detail geometry. Existing tests continue to check closed suspension-bay walls, front suspension mounts and nose taper. Source and prepared browser assets must be regenerated together after changes.

The pressure/return installation has dedicated checks for port contact, tangent alignment, hose separation, bend curvature, full-radius surface clearance, supported carriers and shared detail geometry. The existing closed-wall regression remains enforced after the new service cuts. The whole-car views show the installation. Following the existing installed-only loom pattern, the hydraulic detail frames the rear pack without long remote lines shrinking it; the engine view also omits the front-running branches while retaining the rear manifold fittings. A shared two-line gland replaces the thin carbon web between the bay-entry holes. The next steering work is accurate torque sensing and internal metering/gallery detail. The broader goal also retains active-aero plumbing, remote brake-control plumbing, fire-system plumbing, remaining ancillary components and a full packaging audit. The existing external wing comparisons, airflow illustration and single-viewport studio remain in place.

The cockpit and pedal geometry is now described in [cockpit-reconstruction-notes.md](cockpit-reconstruction-notes.md). The installed wheel and its boss keep the same column connection; the cockpit detail now also includes that shared wheel.
