# Mercedes W17 reference review

Updated 16 October 2026. The page uses real Three.js solids and neutral materials. Photographs are references, not model skins. This remains a photographic reconstruction, not Mercedes manufacturing CAD; passing geometry checks does not establish 1:1 fidelity. The latest whole-exterior audit and its remaining discrepancies are recorded at the end of this document.

FIA Issue 20, C5.4.5 fixes the crank axis 90 mm above the reference plane; C9.5.1 places the secondary shaft above it, 90–110 mm away. The model uses its floor datum at 56 mm above the scene ground, a coaxial crank/clutch/input shaft, and 100 mm shaft separation. The lowered engine has a shallower dry sump; its connected fuel rails, injectors, plenum and exhaust entries follow the revised position.
- The eight gear pairs now occupy a longitudinal cluster starting 420 mm ahead of the differential. Continuous toothed solids replace individually attached box teeth. Bearing collars, selector forks and barrel, dog rings, oil rail and jets sit inside a revised carbon casing. The bell housing has an open front, a bounded lower surface and fasteners; the service seam follows the actual case surface.
- The differential axis is 270 mm above the model reference plane, with angled half-shafts and inboard joint housings. Exact gear tooth counts, selector internals, final-drive tooth form, casing sections, engine casting and all hidden mounting details remain illustrative. Meeting selected regulation dimensions does not establish Mercedes 1:1 fidelity or full regulatory compliance.

The extra detail did not increase text on the main canvas. Small components now fit their isolated viewport without the previous zoom cap; their service controls remain in the existing component panel.

## Brake assembly pass

- [Mercedes' W17 specification](https://www.mercedesamgf1.com/f1-w17-2026-technical-specifications) names **Carbone Industries carbon/carbon discs and pads**, with **Brembo nickel-plated aluminium monobloc calipers**. Brembo's public 2026 photographs are a construction reference, not evidence that the W17 uses Brembo friction discs or the exact pictured caliper.
- FIA Issue 20 C11.3 permits 325–345 mm front discs, 260–280 mm rear discs, at most 34 mm thickness, and cooling bores at least 2.5 mm across. The old equal 316 mm discs were wrong. This reconstruction uses 340 × 32 mm front and 280 × 28 mm rear, 2.6 mm radial bores, six opposing pistons and two pads per caliper. The chosen dimensions, 1080/560 channel counts and piston layout are **not confirmed W17 measurements**.
- The friction ring is a continuous solid with real radial through-bores and unbroken friction faces. Cooling ducts are hollow and pass through openings in the inboard heat shield.
- The hollow carbon shroud belongs to the stationary brake assembly. Its outer face occludes spoke gaps; the former solid drum incorrectly rotated with the wheel. The friction disc and bell now follow wheel rotation, while the caliper and shroud stay fixed. The wheels retain their ten-spoke faces. These parts are still illustrative, not a factory brake CAD assembly.
- The component view fits one front or rear assembly and offers «Снять кожух». The same opened geometry appears in «Как работает».
